Student Guide

Useful information before moving in.

Compare more than the monthly rate.

Ask what is included in your rent, when payments are due, how utilities work and whether any deposits apply.

Make a list of essentials.

Think about your study routine, storage needs, the level of privacy you prefer and whether sharing is right for you.

Plan your daily route.

Check your transport to campus, nearby services and the journey home after late classes.

Confirm the details in writing.

Understand your agreement, residence rules and whom to contact if anything needs attention.
