FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions, clear answers.

Everything you need to know before choosing a room.

How do I apply?+

Use the online application form, choose your preferred residence and room type, and submit your details. The team will contact you about next steps.

Can I choose my Bloemfontein location?+

Yes. You can specify Willows, Universitas, Brandwag or Wesdene, or choose any location.

What types of rooms are available?+

Single and sharing-room options are listed on the site. Exact vacancies and arrangements depend on the residence and date.

Is Uni Residentia accredited with UFS?+

Uni Residentia is UFS-accredited, as stated by the accommodation provider. Contact the team for accreditation details relating to your room or property.

How much does accommodation cost?+

Current rates and any deposits are confirmed when you enquire. We do not publish a price until it is verified for your preferred option.

Can I arrange a viewing?+

Yes. Use the contact form and choose 'Viewing request' so the team can discuss suitable arrangements.

Does submitting an application reserve a room?+

No. A submission is an application request and does not guarantee an offer or confirmed reservation.

How is my personal information used?+

We use your details to review applications and answer enquiries. See the Privacy Notice for more information.
