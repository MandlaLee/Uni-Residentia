Privacy Notice

How we handle your information.

Information we collect

When you use the application or contact form, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, study information, accommodation preference and message.

Why we collect it

We use this information to review accommodation enquiries, respond to questions and communicate about applications.

Where it goes

Submitted information is stored using a third-party hosting and database service. Access to submitted applications and enquiries is restricted through database permissions and row-level security.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your information by submitting a privacy request through the contact form. We may retain information where required by applicable law.

Children and accuracy

Applicants should provide accurate information. Where consent from a parent or guardian is legally required, they should be involved in submitting it.
