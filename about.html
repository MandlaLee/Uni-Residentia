About Uni Residentia

Student accommodation in Bloemfontein.

Our approach

Uni Residentia operates student accommodation in Bloemfontein, serving students who need comfortable, practical spaces during their studies.

Our residences are in Willows, Universitas, Brandwag and Wesdene. We put day-to-day student needs first: a place to focus, a place to unwind and people to turn to when you need help.
