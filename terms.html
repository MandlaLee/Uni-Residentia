Website Terms.

Important information about applications and website content.

Using this website

This website introduces accommodation options in Bloemfontein and allows you to submit enquiries and applications.

Applications and bookings

An online form submission is a request for review. A room is not reserved until Uni Residentia confirms an offer and you accept any applicable agreement.

Rates and availability

Availability and charges may change. Confirm the current rate, included facilities, deposit terms and residence rules directly with the team before making a payment.

Images and information

Accommodation images on this site are illustrative. Actual rooms and shared facilities may vary. We aim to keep information correct, but please verify material details before making decisions.

Enquiries

For questions, use our contact form.
